Our promise

Rules we hold ourselves to.

Every Xspace advisor signs these before they can work with a client.

Only verified inventory

We pitch a property only after it passes verification. If something changes, such as the price, availability or a legal issue, we tell you the same day.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask us.

Have a question that isn't here? Ask us on WhatsApp. A real person replies.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do I pay anything for the shortlist or site visits?

No. The shortlist, video walkthroughs and site visits are free. Your advisor will explain any fees for closing a deal before you commit to anything.

What does "verified" actually mean?

Before we list a property, we check its survey number, approvals, title status and, where it applies, its TG RERA registration. Listings with incomplete documents are not verified, and we don't show them to you.

I live outside Hyderabad or abroad. Can you still help?

Yes. Many buyers start with our video walkthroughs and a live video-call site visit. We can coordinate the paperwork remotely and plan an in-person visit for when you're in town.

Will I get calls from lots of different agents?

No. You get one advisor. Your details stay inside Xspace and are never sold or shared.

Do you help with home loans?

We help you understand what you can borrow, show you bank-approved projects, and coordinate with your lender. The loan itself is approved by the bank.

Can I sell or list my property with you?

Yes. Share the property details and documents with us. Once it passes verification, we present it to buyers looking for that kind of home.
